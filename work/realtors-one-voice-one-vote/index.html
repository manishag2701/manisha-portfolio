Realtors One Voice One Vote

Educational content and training program for a real estate advocacy initiative.

  • Client: Realtors One Voice One Vote
  • 2024
  • Instructional Designer & Curriculum Developer

Overview

Created educational content and training materials for Realtors One Voice One Vote, an advocacy initiative within the real estate sector. The work involved designing a curriculum that educates realtors on civic engagement, voting rights, and advocacy strategies, making the content both informative and motivating.

What I did

  • Designed educational content focused on civic engagement and advocacy
  • Built training materials tailored to the realtor community
  • Created motivating, action-oriented learning experiences

Impact

Training program launched for realtor community
